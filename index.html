📡 TWISTED Radar Signatures

  • Hook Echo: Classic comma-shaped inflow notch — strong tornado potential.
  • BWER: Donut-like weak center surrounded by purple/red — intense updraft.
  • V-Notch: V-shaped hail/wind split downwind of a violent supercell.
  • Purple / White Core: Extreme hail & destructive downdraft winds.

🌪️ EF Scale Quick Reference

  • EF0–EF1 (65–110 mph): Light to moderate damage; safe for close probe drops.
  • EF2–EF3 (111–165 mph): Significant damage; stay ahead & perpendicular.
  • EF4–EF5 (166–200+ mph): Violent wedge; deploy probes early and escape to Safe Zone!
